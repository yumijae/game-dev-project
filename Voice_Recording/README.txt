RECOMMENDED RECORDING SPECS:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Format: WAV (16-bit or 24-bit PCM)
- Sample Rate: 44.1 kHz or 48 kHz
- Channels: Mono (recommended for 3D in-game audio) or Stereo

Whenever you drop new files into these folders, just let me know and I will 
automatically hook them into the Godot audio system and subtitle triggers!
