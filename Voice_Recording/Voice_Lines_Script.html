HULING KAMPANA (The Last Bell)

Official Voice Recording Script & Director's Guide
Character: Juan Dela Cruz • Setting: STI College Kalibo (4 Floors + Rooftop) • Timeline: 11:45 PM to 5:30 AM
Core Premise: Juan fell asleep working late. The power has tripped, a storm is battering the windows, and the main security gate is chained shut. In the dark, three folklore entities hunt through sound, scent, and mimicry.
🎙️ Microphone & Room Setup Record 3–5 inches from the mic in a quiet space (e.g., closet with clothes around you). Juan is trapped in tight classrooms; avoid room echo for maximum intimacy.
🤫 Suppressed Delivery (Whispers) Speak softly with heavy breath. In the lore, making noise attracts the Aswang. If you yell, the creature hears you. Keep urgency tight and suppressed.
⚡ Panic Arc Progression Start at 11:45 PM groggy & confused → uneasy as footsteps echo → hands shaking at 3:00 AM → desperate prayer & pure survival adrenaline at dawn.
💾 Export Settings Save as WAV (16-bit or 24-bit, 44.1 kHz or 48 kHz). Mono or Stereo are both fine. Drop files directly into Voice_Recording/.
